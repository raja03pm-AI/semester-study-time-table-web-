Final revision pattern

Three passes, no panic

  1. Pass 1 — repair: revisit missed concepts and finish notes.
  2. Pass 2 — retrieve: solve past questions or self-test without notes.
  3. Pass 3 — compress: formulas, definitions, diagrams and weak spots.
Exam eve: stop adding content early, prepare essentials, then sleep.
Reality check

Starting from zero and completing 40 units by 15 Nov means roughly 2–3 units every day. It is a stretch goal, not a reason to rush shallowly. Keep the high-priority courses moving first; use the final-revision blocks to repair any unfinished medium-priority unit.
